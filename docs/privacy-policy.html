Privacy Policy

Daily Motivation · Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Motivation mobile application (“the app”) handles information.

Information the app collects

Daily Motivation does not ask you to create an account and does not collect or send your personal information to the developer. The current version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your saved favorite quotes and a small preference used to remember whether an in-app hint has been shown. This information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove this information.

Notifications

The app schedules a daily motivation reminder on your device. Android may ask for permission to show notifications. Reminder scheduling is handled locally by your device; the app does not send your notification information to the developer.

Sharing

If you choose to share a quote, Android opens the system sharing interface so you can select another app. The quote image or content is shared only through the app you choose. That app's own privacy policy and practices apply to information you share with it.

Third-party services

The app does not currently use its own servers to collect personal information. When you use Android or another app to share content, those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

Daily Motivation does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Security and changes

Because the app's saved preferences remain on your device, keep your device secured. This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be published at this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact information provided on the Daily Motivation Google Play Store listing.
